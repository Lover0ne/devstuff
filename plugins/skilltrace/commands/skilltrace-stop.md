---
name: skilltrace-stop
description: Use when user wants to stop or pause Skilltrace. "stop skilltrace", "pause tracking", "disable skilltrace", "stop tracking for now".
---

Stop Skilltrace tracking:
1. Run: `bash "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/hooks/wrapper.sh" stop`
2. Confirm: "Skilltrace stopped. Use /skilltrace-start to resume tracking."
